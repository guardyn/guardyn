2. Service Description

Guardyn is a privacy-focused end-to-end encrypted messaging platform. The service includes:

  • 1-on-1 messaging with end-to-end encryption (X3DH + Double Ratchet)
  • Group chat with OpenMLS encryption (up to 1000 members)
  • Media sharing (photos, videos, files up to 10GB)
  • Voice and video calls with SFrame E2EE
  • Live broadcasting with E2EE streams
  • Self-hosted deployment options (Community Edition)
  • Enterprise features (LDAP, SAML, compliance tools - Enterprise Edition)

Guardyn is currently in MVP/PoC phase. Features may change, and service availability is not guaranteed during development.

4. Privacy and Security

Guardyn implements end-to-end encryption for all messages and media. This means:

  • Only you and your intended recipients can read your messages
